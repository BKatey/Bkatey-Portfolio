How to play Word Search

  1. Look for a word from the list in the letters.
  2. Put your finger (or mouse) on the first letter and slide to the last letter. Or tap the first letter, then tap the last.
  3. The word gets crossed out and you'll hear it. Then say it out loud yourself!

Keyboard: use the arrow keys to move and press Enter on the first and last letter.

    How to play Crossword

    1. Tap a clue or a square. The word you're working on turns blue.
    2. Read the clue or press 🔊 to hear the word, then type it.
    3. Tap the same square again to switch between Across and Down.
    4. Stuck? Open 🛟 Help me to sound it out or check your letters.

        Which words to use

        Add a clue after a colon, like glacier: A huge, slow-moving river of ice. Words already in the built-in list get their clue, syllables and "say it" guide automatically. Use at least 4 words, each 3 to 14 letters.
